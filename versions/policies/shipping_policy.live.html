SHIPPING POLICY

WHERE WE SHIP
We ship to all 50 US states and Washington, DC. We don't currently ship to US territories (such as Puerto Rico or Guam), APO/FPO military addresses, or outside the United States.

SHIPPING RATES
• Orders under $50: $5.95 flat rate
• Orders $50 and over: FREE shipping

PROCESSING AND DELIVERY TIMES
Orders are processed within 1–3 business days. Most orders arrive within about 8 days of being placed. During holidays and sale periods, please allow a few extra days. Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees.

Our products ship from our fulfillment partners' warehouses. If you order more than one item, your order may arrive in separate packages.

TRACKING
When your order ships, we'll email you a tracking number. Tracking can take 1–2 days to start updating.

DELAYED OR LOST PACKAGES
If your order hasn't arrived within 14 days, email support@zalinbeauty.com and we'll look into it with the carrier. If your package is confirmed lost, or hasn't arrived within 30 days of your order, we'll send a free replacement or give you a full refund.

PACKAGE MARKED "DELIVERED" BUT NOT RECEIVED
Please check around your property and with neighbors, and wait 24 hours, as carriers sometimes mark packages delivered early. If it still hasn't turned up, contact us within 7 days and we'll help you file a claim with the carrier.

WRONG OR INCOMPLETE ADDRESS
Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. We can't be responsible for orders delayed or lost because of an incorrect or incomplete address. If you spot a mistake, email us right away. We can update it if the order hasn't shipped yet.

QUESTIONS
Email support@zalinbeauty.com. We reply within 1–2 business days.
