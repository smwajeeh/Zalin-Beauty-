REFUND POLICY

We want you to love your ZALIN Beauty purchase. If something isn't right, here's how we'll help.

30-DAY RETURNS
You can request a return within 30 days of delivery. To be eligible, the item must be unused, in its original condition and packaging, with all accessories, and you'll need your order number.

To start a return, email support@zalinbeauty.com with your order number and the reason for the return. Please don't send items back without contacting us first; we'll reply with the return address and instructions. Items sent back without a return request can't be accepted.

ITEMS WE CAN'T ACCEPT BACK
For hygiene reasons, beauty devices that have been used on the skin, face, hair or lashes can't be returned unless they're faulty. This includes facial massagers, skin scrubbers, pore cleansers, eyelash curlers and LED masks.

RETURN SHIPPING
Return shipping is paid by the customer, unless the item arrived damaged, defective or wrong. We recommend a tracked shipping service, because we can't refund items that don't reach us. Original shipping charges are not refundable.

DAMAGED, DEFECTIVE OR WRONG ITEMS
If your order arrives damaged, stops working under normal use, or isn't what you ordered, email support@zalinbeauty.com within 7 days of delivery with your order number and clear photos or a short video of the problem and the packaging. We'll send a free replacement or give you a full refund. In most cases you won't need to send the item back.

REFUNDS
Once we receive and check your return, we'll email you to confirm. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5 business days. Your bank or card provider may take a few extra days to show it.

BUNDLES AND DISCOUNTS
If you bought a bundle or used a discount and return part of the order, we'll refund the amount you actually paid for the returned item.

EXCHANGES
The fastest way to get a different item or color is to return the one you have and place a new order.

QUESTIONS
Email us any time at support@zalinbeauty.com. We reply within 1–2 business days.
