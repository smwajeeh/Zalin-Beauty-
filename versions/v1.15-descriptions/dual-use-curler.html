How to use

  1. Start with dry, brushed hair. A heat protectant is recommended.
  2. Choose a heat setting. Start lower for fine or damaged hair.
  3. To straighten: clamp a small section near the roots and glide down slowly.
  4. To curl: clamp a section, twist the styler half a turn and glide down.
  5. Let curls cool before touching them.

Please read the safety instructions included with your styler before first use. Keep away from water and out of reach of children.
